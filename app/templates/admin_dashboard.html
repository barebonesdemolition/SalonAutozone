Delivery fees

Delivery is priced by distance from the shop's map pin to the buyer's pin: starting charge + price per km, never less than the minimum, rounded to Le 5. Bulky parts go by van at a multiple of the okada price.

Fixed fee when there's no map pin (optional)

Used when the shop or the buyer hasn't dropped a pin. Leave empty to charge a typical 10 km trip.

Take a share of delivery fees too (normally 0)

This is how SalonAutoZone earns while you grow. Buyers pay SalonAutoZone everything; after delivery the rider gets the whole delivery fee and the shop gets the parts price less your commission. When you move to monthly shop fees, set this to 0.
